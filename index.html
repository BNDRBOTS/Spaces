Nearby information

Prime Business District

Positioned in the heart of Downtown Phoenix, 101 East Washington Street places you in the city's thriving Central Business District. Surrounded by corporate offices, government agencies, and professional services, this location is ideal for companies seeking flexible office space. Enjoy the convenience of nearby Phoenix City Hall and the Arizona State Capitol, making it a strategic hub for businesses interacting with civic and state institutions. This vibrant area ensures you're always close to opportunity.

Exceptional Transport Links

Seamlessly connected, this location benefits from excellent transport options. The Valley Metro Rail runs through the Central Avenue corridor, linking Downtown Phoenix with surrounding neighborhoods. For international or domestic travel, Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport is just a short drive away. Whether commuting locally or traveling globally, your workspace ensures easy access. Convenience and connectivity come standard here, letting you focus on your business without missing a beat.

Cultural and Recreational Gems

Immerse yourself in Downtown Phoenix's rich cultural scene just steps from your office. Explore the Arizona Science Center for inspiration or unwind at Margaret T. Hance Park, a lush green space perfect for a midday recharge. The historic Orpheum Theatre and Heritage Square add a touch of artistic flair, while the Phoenix Convention Center hosts major events year-round. This dynamic environment blends work and play effortlessly, energizing your daily routine.

Shopping and Dining Hub

Your workspace is surrounded by retail and entertainment options at CityScape Phoenix, a vibrant mixed-use complex anchoring the downtown area. From stylish boutiques to trendy eateries, everything you need is right at your fingertips. Whether grabbing a quick lunch or hosting a client dinner, the area's diverse culinary offerings cater to every taste. This prime location ensures your business feels at the center of it all, with convenience woven into every aspect of your day.
